About LiveLong AI

AI-powered real-time healthcare decision system for India — built to be useful, explainable, and accessible.

🎯 Problem Statement

Millions of people in India struggle to make basic healthcare decisions — whether to go to a government or private hospital, how much it will cost, and whether their condition is urgent. LiveLong AI solves this by providing an intelligent, explainable, budget-aware care recommendation system in real time.

⚙️ Decision Intelligence Pipeline

1
Symptom Input — User describes symptoms in plain English or uses quick-add chips
2
Keyword Matching — 24 symptom patterns matched against a structured database
3
Emergency Detection — 20+ critical keywords trigger instant emergency override
4
Condition Inference — Symptom cluster matched to 15 known medical conditions
5
Confidence Scoring — Match strength, data coverage, and specificity combined into a 0–1 score
6
Budget Analysis — Govt vs Private cost compared to user's budget; shortfall calculated
7
Hospital Ranking — Top 3 hospitals scored on type match, rating, budget fit, and proximity
8
Explanation + Advice — Conversational explanation + human-like advice + risk insight generated

🤖 Responsible AI Principles

  • Uses suggestion / recommendation language — never "diagnosis" or "prescription"
  • Always includes an explanation of why a decision was made
  • Displays confidence scores so users understand certainty levels
  • Shows uncertainty notice on every result
  • Mandatory disclaimer bar visible on all pages
  • Emergency situations always routed to professional care, never self-treatment

⚠️ Disclaimer

LiveLong AI provides decision support only. It is not a medical diagnosis tool and does not replace professional medical advice, consultation, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions. In emergencies, call 108 immediately.
